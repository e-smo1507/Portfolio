About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science Engineering student at Dr. Ambedkar Institute of Technology for Divyangjan, Kanpur, focused on Machine Learning, Generative AI, and NLP. My work centers on turning raw, unstructured problems — a messy PDF, a plain-English question, an open research topic — into structured, automated systems.

Over the past year I've built multi-agent research pipelines with LangChain, natural-language-to-SQL interfaces, document Q&A agents, and content-based recommenders — most of them shipped as live, usable applications rather than notebooks. I care about the full pipeline: data, retrieval, reasoning, and the interface that makes it usable.

Alongside AI/ML, I build with the MERN-adjacent frontend stack — React, Tailwind, and vanilla JS — which lets me take a model from a Python script to a deployed product end to end.
